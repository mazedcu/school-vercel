How to install

  1. Tap “Download for Android”If your browser asks whether to keep the file, choose Download or Keep.
  2. Open the downloaded fileTap the download notification, or find RadonAcademy-{{ app.version }}.apk in your Files app under Downloads.
  3. Allow the install if askedAndroid may say apps from this source aren't allowed. Tap Settings, turn on “Allow from this source”, go back and tap Install.
  4. Open Radon Academy and log inUse the same username and password as the website. Allow notifications to hear about new results.
